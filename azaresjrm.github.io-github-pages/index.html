TrackIT

Inter-office document tracking for the University of San Agustin.

TrackIT helps selected university offices record, route, and follow official documents through a centralized browser-based workflow. It was a group academic capstone; I served as the primary developer of the actual system.

4.26 / 5Overall acceptance meanAcademic evaluation · 12 selected office respondents · “Excellent” in the study
View live demo ↗Explore case study ↓
01

The problem

Paper logbooks, separate records, verbal updates, and repeated follow-ups made it difficult to know a document's current office, status, and most recent action.

02

The solution

A centralized application that records document details, assigns reference codes, routes items between offices, and preserves status history with timestamps.

03

Key features

Role-based interfaces, outgoing and incoming queues, attachments, receive/return/forward/complete actions, logs, notifications, search, and reports.

04

Development

The academic project followed an iterative Agile process covering requirements, system design, implementation, testing, feedback, and refinement.
